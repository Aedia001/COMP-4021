⛏️ Gold Miner Dual PK ⛏️
A Two-Player Mining Competition!

Race against another miner to collect the most valuable treasures underground! Launch your hook at the right moment to grab gold, diamonds, and more. But watch out — your opponent can steal items right from your hook!

🎮 How to Play

  • Press SPACE to launch your mining hook
  • The hook swings automatically — time your press carefully!
  • Catch gold nuggets, diamonds, and other items for points
  • You can steal items the other player is currently catching!
  • Avoid bombs (-100 pts)!
  • Game lasts 2 minutes — highest score wins!

💰 Items & Values

🥇 Small Gold — 50 pts 🏆 Big Gold — 200 pts 💎 Diamond — 500 pts 🪨 Rock — 10 pts (heavy!) 💣 Bomb — -100 pts
